## 👋 About

I'm a **Senior Software Engineer** in Bengaluru with **3+ years** of hands-on experience shipping backend-heavy products across **retail, telecom and PLM**. Today I'm building **HRMS and clinical software at Epicure AI Labs**, after earlier years at Wipro working on Nokia NSP, Oracle Cloud Fusion and Enovia PLM 3DX.

I like turning messy, real-world problems into calm, dependable systems — and I'm increasingly pointing **AI agents** at the repetitive work that eats a team's time.
